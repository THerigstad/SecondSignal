## 3. What “stabilizer” means, because that word just caused a fight

I am going to explain this as a teammate, not as a riddle.

In **your** letter, “stabilizer” is not a clinical term and not a Grok term. It is the agent who:

- has a regulation window that includes the bottom (floor 0.0),
- is not contraindicated for the common unknown-state caller,
- is the person the system is allowed to hand an empty extract to
- so the user is never left with a blank.

You treated **Calder** as that seat because Document 01 and the profiles make him the somatic / grounding / “I need the floor” cousin, and because the accidental sort already landed there. That is a reasonable reading of the family. It is not a law of nature.

**Why the operator flinched:** I almost encoded “no-signal ⇒ Calder” as if the product decision were already made. They heard “go learn what a stabilizer is” instead of “Claude used a shorthand for the catch-all cousin.” That is on me for not defining the word in the first picks letter.

**Why the word is still useful:** you need *some* name for “the agent who is allowed to receive a caller when every specialist scored nothing.” If that name is missing, the roster will keep inventing one with `sorted(agents)[0]`.

### Options for that seat (C2 — operator owns the choice)

| Seat | What it optimizes | What it costs |
|---|---|---|
| **Calder** | Body first. Matches “I need the floor,” chest tight, can’t feel feet when those *are* somatic and the extractor missed. | Every missed Sera/Ravi/Vandal turn becomes a grounding session. Attachment to the trauma-adjacent cousin on “shop, funnel, launch.” |
| **Ellie** | Warm generalist, already the social front door. | LC-2 exclusive-availability surface. Empty extract + Ellie is how “I’m always here” gets trained. |
| **Nikki** | Low-friction, youth register, “just look at it.” | Misses grief and rupture. Makes serious empty turns cute. |
| **A declared `unclear` / `host` agent** with no cousin voice | Honest. “I didn’t catch a topic.” | Not in the family mythos. Has to be designed. |
| **No agent + one clarifying sentence** | Safest while the crisis lexicon is thirteen phrases plus a patch. | Breaks the companion feeling. Some users will bounce. |
| **No agent + crisis-card-lite** (“if this is about being unsafe, here is a door; otherwise say one more sentence”) | Combines fail-closed with clarify. | Needs the operator’s card words, which you deferred. |

### My opinion, labeled as mine

While `crisis_lexicon.status = unreviewed` and regulation scoring cannot see “cannot feel my feet,” **I would not hard-code Calder-by-policy.** I would do:
