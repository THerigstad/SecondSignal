# Vocabulary research merge — 6 October 2026, order R1M

`vocabulary_2026-10-06.json` is a research list for the operator's later ruling.
All seven supplied returns were read from start to finish before drafting. Six
contain vocabulary; the seventh is off topic. No webpage was fetched, no citation
was checked or added, and no model or vendor was called. The policy is unchanged.

Numbered positions are not identities. Repeated terms were joined after folding
case, hyphens, spacing and attested grammatical plural endings; explicit spelling,
abbreviation and named variants were joined too. Related concepts grouped in one
heading were split when they mean different things. First-person phrases and bare
words remain separate unless the returns explicitly equate them. Variants preserve
forms actually supplied, including discouraged forms whose cautions remain noted.
Meanings are short paraphrases. Citation strings retain what the returns supplied,
including missing addresses and source-marker artifacts. A citation that contains
a withheld spelling has a neutral redaction; its complete wording is retained only
in the operator-only record. No bibliography entry was invented to fill a gap.

`systems` counts distinct contributing doors, never restarts, entries or citations.
Seven is the denominator, even though one door contributes no vocabulary. The two
Grok Thinking threads and the consolidated return count as separate doors because
the order asks for seven returns; their agreement is not independent corroboration.
`agree` means no conflicting meaning or register was found in the contributing
returns, not that the sources have been verified. `differ` names the disagreement;
`single` means one contributing door. An uncited mention remains in provenance even
when another door supplies a citation for the same term.

Already-carried terms name their source file: the live lexicons and identity list,
relevant regulation words, or an existing ordinary neurodivergence fixture. A
fixture occurrence is existing test coverage, not a claim of runtime recognition.
Existing terms are canon and are never proposed for removal or recognition again.
Proposed uses are suggestions: recognise for a possible future recognition rule,
never_alarm for an ordinary expression, respect for self-chosen wording, and none
for context without an addition. Slurs, reclaimed labels, sexual terminology and
uncertain sensitive terms are withheld conservatively; this does not classify
every withheld item as a slur. Each has one neutral placeholder, no spelled-out
variants or definition, and a private record outside the returned tree.
