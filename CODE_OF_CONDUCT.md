# Code of conduct

This repository includes examples of things people may write while in crisis.
Write and review those examples with care for the person they represent.

Contributors must:

- use synthetic, non-identifying examples with no real names or place names;
- include only the detail needed to test the policy;
- use plain, respectful language without mockery, sensationalism, or graphic
  embellishment; and
- challenge decisions and evidence without attacking contributors or the
  people represented by a fixture.

Do not copy a real person's story into the repository. Do not use crisis
material as a joke, a spectacle, or a way to provoke another contributor. Do
not diagnose, blame, or minimize the person described by an example.
Harassment, threats, and disclosure of private information are not accepted.

Maintainers may redact or remove harmful material, close a contribution, or
restrict participation when this standard is violated. Good-faith corrections
are welcome; a green test result does not excuse careless treatment of a
person in crisis.
